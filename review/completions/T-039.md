## Status: COMPLETED
## Changes
- File: `crates/al-semantic/src/host.rs`, `crates/al-semantic/src/lib.rs`
- What: Changed `call` to take `&mut self` (or made `DotNetHost` `pub(crate)`); changed `cache` and `host` modules to `pub(crate)`; added documentation noting that the 30s timeout does not release the Mutex lock.

## Justification
